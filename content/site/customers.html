JANUARY · A CARD TAP

She buys a pair of hurricane lamps in Savannah. You already know a little.

Lucía is visiting her daughter and doesn't give her name. The name on her card links the sale to a new customer record. When she orders from magnigift.com.mx in February with the same phone number, the two purchases join up in one record.

SHOPPER RECOGNITION

Strangers become names

Link counter sales to customers by email, phone or the name on the card, and see how well you know each one.

CUSTOMER PROFILE

Everything on one page

Lifetime spend, average order, last visit, preferences and live stats in one pane.

DUPLICATE FINDER

One person, one record

Records that share a phone number or card are flagged, and one merge moves everything over.

1 more way to meet a customer
Quick add. Add someone in seconds for a phone order; they can claim the record online later.
MARCH · SHE SIGNS UP

One invitation, in Spanish, and she has an account.

Sofía Herrera, who manages the San Miguel de Allende store, sends Lucía an invitation from her record. Lucía sets up her account, ticks the box for news, and the welcome email arrives in Spanish with the offer from the magnigift.com.mx pop-up.

ACCOUNT INVITATIONS

From regular to member in a click

Invite a customer you already know to set up an online account, in one click.

SIGN-UP FORMS

A pop-up that minds its manners

A footer form and a polite pop-up that shows the offer from your welcome email, so the two always match.

EMAIL FLOWS

The right email at the right time

Welcome, abandoned cart, review, win-back, browsing and back-in-stock emails, with splits and waits you set once.

3 more sign-up tools
Subscriber list. Everyone who agreed to marketing, with a record of each person's consent; import and export.
Safe email change. An account's email only changes once the customer confirms the new address.
Text messages. Send receipts, invitations and updates by text.
MAY · EMAILS SHE OPENS

Your spring campaign shows each customer the brands they buy, in their country.

Priya Natarajan, Magnigift's marketing lead, sends to everyone in Mexico who bought table linens in the last year. Lucía's email shows what's in stock that morning, at her price in pesos, and she isn't sent a second email that week.

MARKETING EMAIL

Campaigns from your own name

Send from your own domain, with a live preview, a test send and scheduling.

AUDIENCE BUILDER

Send to the right people

Choose by spend, last visit, brands bought, country, group and tags, with a live count as you go.

LIVE PRODUCT BLOCKS

Never email a sold-out item

Products are filled in when the email sends. Sold-out items drop out, and each person sees their own price.

3 more email tools
Subject-line test. Try two subject lines on a slice of the list; the winner goes to everyone else.
Smart sending. Nobody is emailed twice within the window you choose.
Customer email editor. Rewrite any email your customers receive, with merge fields and a real preview.
AUGUST · A QUESTION

"¿Me pueden apartar el platón azul para recogerlo en Savannah?"

Lucía replies to an order email, asking to hold a blue platter for her next trip. It lands in Magnigift's shared inbox. Marcus Bell in Savannah picks it up, and a note on her record means whoever serves her, in either country, knows about the platter.

SHARED TEAM INBOX

Every question has an owner

Customer conversations sit in department queues, each assigned to someone with a clear status.

REPLIES THREAD IN

Replies land in the right place

A customer's reply joins the right conversation, old quoted text removed, and the thread reopens.

TAGS AND NOTES

What your team should know

Tags, notes and a status on each customer, for staff only. The customer never sees them.

1 more team tool
Team directory and chat. Everyone's role and store, plus direct conversations with colleagues.
DECEMBER · MEMBER AND REFERRER

She's Gold now, three friends shop with you, and her daughter's studio has a trade account.

Lucía earns points in Savannah, in San Miguel and online, moved up a tier in the autumn, and shared her referral link. Her daughter's interior design studio in Savannah opened a trade account of its own, with terms and its own buyers.

LOYALTY WITH TIERS

One rewards program, everywhere

Points in store and online, tiers that earn faster, and a warning email before points expire.

REFERRALS

Friends who bring friends

Give a reward, get a reward, with checks against abuse. Credit arrives once the return window has passed.

CUSTOMER GROUPS

Prices for the people you choose

Group pricing, and requests to join a group approved right where they arrive.

4 more tools for trade customers
Trade accounts. Separate business accounts with payment terms, a credit limit, their own price list and the amount owed.
Buyer roles and order limits. Company buyers, approvers and viewers, each with their own order limit.
Tax exemption. Store each customer's certificate and expiry date; an expired exemption stops applying.
VAT number check. Check a business's EU VAT number before you trade.
