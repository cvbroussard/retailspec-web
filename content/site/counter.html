Saturday rush in four stores. Savannah's Wi-Fi just dropped. Savannah keeps selling.

When you run counters in four cities, two countries and a tasting room, a good day is won or lost at every one of them at once. RetailSpec's registers, screens and store floors run from the same catalogue as your websites, so no counter ever has to wait on anything.

08:55 · OPENING

Every screen in every store is already showing the right thing.

Before the first customer walks in, counter screens, window displays and kiosks in Charleston, Savannah, Asheville and San Miguel have woken up with today's promotions, in English or Spanish. In Paso Robles, the Cielo Verde tasting room menu shows today's pours. Nobody touched a USB stick.

PANORAMA

Every device, one map

See every register, printer and screen in every store on one map, and preview each screen on its real hardware before it goes live.

SIGNAGE

Screens that run themselves

Playlists scheduled by time of day ("weekdays 7–11: breakfast"), wall menus with live prices, and they keep playing offline.

STORE HOURS

Knows when you're open

Signage loops while a drawer is open and shows your logo, or goes dark, when you're closed.

6 more ways the store opens itself
Pair by code. A TV stick shows a 6-character code; type it in and the screen pairs itself.
One link per device. Each device opens only the apps it's allowed.
Installable register. Installs like an app and reopens without a connection.
Customer display. An idle slideshow of promotions, rewards and gift cards between sales.
Branded card reader. Card readers sleep on your logo and welcome line.
Kiosk, shop or showroom. Customers buy, or browse and tap "Ask a team member".
11:42 · THE RUSH

The Savannah line is six deep. Nothing slows down.

Dana Pruitt, the Savannah manager, rings up a regular by phone number, the candle deal applies itself, and the card tap is instant because the register talks straight to the reader on the store network. Next in line is a hotel buyer from the trade program, and her price list applies on its own.

SPECPICK TECHNOLOGY

Browses like your website

Categories, brands and collections scoped to what's on this store's shelves, with the register's best sellers up front. It learns which sections each register uses most.

CUSTOMER BAR

One field finds anyone

Type digits and it searches by phone; type letters and it searches by name. An unknown number becomes a member in one tap.

DEALS

Deals apply themselves

Buy X get Y, mix and match, tiers and spend-and-get apply automatically, each on its own line, the same on the cart and pay screens.

9 more things that keep the line moving
Direct reader link. The cart shows on the card reader and taps are instant.
Scan anything. Items not on this store's list still sell; the sale is flagged to fix later, never blocked.
Group and trade pricing. A customer's price list applies at the counter.
Fast money keypad. Amounts fill from the right, and the keyboard never covers the cart.
Check-in QR. Customers scan the display to join rewards; a photo of the code expires in 90 seconds.
Phone member card. A barcode on the customer's phone that scans like a product.
Gift cards. Sell, reload and check balances, with a magstripe swiper if you have one.
Receipts your way. Print, email or text, with gift receipts and your own wording.
Shared printers. Two registers share one printer, with a backup if the first fails.
14:15 · WHEN IT GOES WRONG

Savannah's internet drops, a return in Asheville has no receipt, a sale gets voided in Charleston.

This is the hour any system is judged by, and with four stores it happens somewhere most weeks. RetailSpec keeps selling, keeps a record of everything, and leaves the decisions to the managers you trust in each store.

OFFLINE

Keeps selling with no internet

Cash and gift card sales queue on the device and sync without duplicates when the connection comes back. Receipts still print.

VOIDS

Void now, review later

Staff void freely so the line keeps moving. The whole sale and the moment it was voided are kept for a manager to check against camera footage.

RETURNS

No-receipt returns, done fairly

Store credit at the lowest price the item sold for in the last 90 days, with a manager PIN, or switched off entirely.

7 more safeguards
Resume a crashed checkout. A reloaded register offers to resume or void; payments reverse automatically.
Discount caps. Each role has a limit; a manager PIN goes over it.
Loss-prevention log. Every void, override, no-sale and refund recorded with a reason, with optional alerts.
PIN security. A removed staff member is locked out at once; five wrong PINs lock the pad.
Auto-lock. The register locks after the idle minutes you choose.
Unfinished sales. Managers see parked, voided and abandoned sales, with who did what.
Training mode. New staff practise on a real register with nothing recorded.
18:05 · CLOSING UP

Four stores count their drawers. Everyone goes home.

In each store, the drawer count, the day's report and the card settlement take minutes. Claire Whitaker's morning summary is waiting in her inbox before she's had coffee.

BLIND COUNT

Honest drawer counts

Staff count without seeing the expected amount. Over and short are recorded, and Z reports are numbered in order.

END-OF-DAY CAPTURE

Free same-day voids

Card payments are captured when the drawer closes, so a same-day void costs nothing and never shows on the customer's statement.

DAILY SUMMARY

Yesterday, before coffee

A morning email: yesterday against the same day last week, in store vs online, and your top products, so an owner sees every store at a glance.

5 more end-of-day tools
X and Z on screen. Read the day without printing.
Cash log. Every pay-in and pay-out with a reason.
Time clock. Staff clock in and out on the tablet; you approve the week and export hours.
Customer capture rate. How many sales had a customer attached, by cashier.
Cashier alerts. Your phone buzzes when a cashier crosses the limits you set.

Opening a fifth store doesn't mean starting over.
