Q1 · WHO CHANGED THIS PRICE?

Every change has a name and a time on it, whichever store it came from.

The linen throw rang up at 58 instead of 68 in Charleston. Claire opens its history, sees Jonah in Savannah changed it at 9:12 during a markdown, and puts it back in one click.

AUDIT LOG

Who changed what

Every edit is recorded with the person, the time, and the value before and after.

RECORD HISTORY

Thirteen months of versions

Every product, customer and setting keeps its past versions for 13 months. Restore any one of them.

CHECKPOINTS

A safe point before big changes

Name a checkpoint before a risky change. RetailSpec takes one on its own before every import.

1 more way to take it back
Undo the restore. Restored the wrong version? Undo that too.
Q2 · WHAT IF WE LOSE EVERYTHING?

Halcourt's data lives in its own home, and it's backed up every night.

Claire has heard the stories: a crashed server, a chain's system gone overnight. For Halcourt, Magnigift and Cielo Verde alike, last night's backup ran at 2:00 and was test-restored by 2:14.

YOUR OWN DATABASE

Never shared with another merchant

Your records sit in a database of their own. No other merchant's data is ever mixed in with yours.

BACKUPS

Backups that are proven to restore

Daily backups, with restores tested so you know they work. You're alerted if a backup is ever late.

CREDENTIALS VAULT

Your keys under lock

The keys to your payments, accounting and other systems are sealed with strong encryption. Screens show only the last 4 characters.

2 more safeguards
Weekly backup summary. A short note each week that your backups ran and restored.
Safe API keys. Keys you issue are stored scrambled, with a scope, an expiry date and a rate limit.
Q3 · WHO CAN SEE WHAT?

Dozens of people, each with their own job. Only Claire sees the margins.

Lucía runs the San Miguel store and can sell and take returns. Dev at the Charleston warehouse can receive stock. Tasting room staff at Cielo Verde can ring up a tasting. Only Claire and her finance lead, Priya Raman, see cost prices and the books. Everyone signs in with a password and a code sent to their email.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS

Decide what each person can do

Set what each person can see and do, all the way down to the register.

SIGN-IN CODES

A password is not enough

Every sign-in needs a password plus a one-time code sent by email.

TRUSTED BROWSERS

Sign out everywhere

Trust a browser for the days you choose. One click signs out every device at once.

2 more account protections
"That wasn't me" lock. If your email address changes, the old address gets a 7-day undo and a one-click lock.
Modern password rules. Long passwords and a list of known-bad ones, following current US government guidance.
Q4 · IS SUPPORT SNOOPING?

We see your business only when you let us in.

A receipt printer in Asheville won't line up. Marcus Bell, the store manager everyone calls for anything with a cable, rings support from inside RetailSpec, gives them a 30-minute code, and watches every page they open until he ends it.

SUPPORT ACCESS

A code you hand out

Support gets in only with a code from you, and only for the time you set.

WATCH THE VISIT

Every page they open

See each page support opens while they're in your account, and end the visit any time.

GET HELP

Ring us from the console

Call support from inside RetailSpec and share your screen, so nobody has to describe what they're looking at.

1 more thing about help calls
Recordings kept 90 days. Help calls are recorded and kept for 90 days, then removed.
Q5 · ARE WE FAIR TO OUR CUSTOMERS?

Your customers trust you. The rules come built in.

A shopper in Toronto signs up for the Magnigift newsletter, one in London browses magnigift.com without saying yes to cookies, and a wine club member unsubscribes from her mail app. Each gets exactly what the law in their region says.

CONSENT BY REGION

Opt-in where the law asks

Marketing follows opt-in rules for the EU, UK, Switzerland and Canada. Every skipped send is logged.

UNSUBSCRIBE

One click, no sign-in

Customers leave your list in one click, including from their mail app's own unsubscribe button.

COOKIE BANNER

Only where it's needed

The banner shows only where the law needs it. One choice covers our cookie, Google Analytics and the Meta pixel.

2 more ways to do right by customers
Policy templates. Returns, privacy and terms drafts for your region, filled in with your details for you to review.
Five languages. Console, storefront, notifications and shopper emails each speak the reader's language.
