A new brand arrives Monday. By Friday it's in four stores and on two websites, in two languages.

With RetailSpec you subscribe to the brand and its whole catalogue lands finished for every store and storefront, then you make it yours.

MON 09:12 · THE BRAND ARRIVES

One subscription. The whole brand is in.

Nadia Brennan, Magnigift's buyer, signed Hollis & Pine at a trade show in Atlanta. On Monday she looks at the brand in the Library, likes what she sees, and subscribes. Copy, photos, sizes and colours land for Charleston, Savannah, Asheville and San Miguel before her coffee is cold.

The mugs sit with the tableware, on both websites and in all four stores.

Nadia groups the four mug colours into one family, drops the new line into "Kitchen & Table", and gives the brand page a hero photo. Both storefronts and every register follow, and each store files the line under its own floor department.

Stay on the right side of the brand, and know if it's paying its way.

Hollis & Pine asked for no discounting below their minimum price. When Nadia sets up a weekend promotion across the four stores, RetailSpec warns her the board is caught in it. The decision stays hers. Claire Whitaker checks the brand scorecard beside the other brands Magnigift carries.
