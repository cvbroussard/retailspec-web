BRING YOUR OWN HARDWARE

Keep the hardware you already have.

Most of what sits on your counters today carries over. If a device meets the standard for its kind, it works with RetailSpec, whoever made it. Moving doesn't have to come with a hardware bill.

KEEP IT

Tablets and screens

Any iPad on iPadOS 17 or later, an Android tablet on Android 12 or later with Google services, or a Windows 11 computer with Chrome or Edge.

KEEP IT

Receipt printers

80 mm receipt printers that speak ESC/POS, on the network or through a print server. A cash drawer that opens from the printer comes along with it.

KEEP IT

Label printers

Label printers that speak ZPL II, at 203 or 300 dpi.

KEEP IT

Barcode scanners

USB or Bluetooth scanners that type like a keyboard and read 2D codes.

KEEP IT

Print servers

A Windows, macOS or Linux computer already in the store, or a browser set up as a print station.

USUALLY CHANGES

Card readers

A card reader that Stripe Terminal supports in your country. Readers tied to another payment processor don't carry over, so this is usually the one thing that changes.

Checked with you before anything moves. Once you subscribe, your migration lead goes through what's on your counters with you, store by store, and confirms what carries over before a single register switches.

CERTIFIED

Proven in our lab

COMPATIBLE

Bring your own

Any device built to the published standard for its kind, including most of what you already own. We haven't proven that exact model in our lab, so help is best effort.

UNSUPPORTED

Known not to fit

Devices we know won't work well with RetailSpec. We list them so nobody buys one by mistake.
