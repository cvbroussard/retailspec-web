09:00 · THE PHONE RINGS

A Savannah hotel wants 240 place settings for its reopening.

Nadia Brooks on the Magnigift order desk takes the call at the hotel's trade price, emails a quote while the purchasing manager is still on the line, and the deposit is paid before she hangs up. Two lines are out of stock, so she adds them as special orders to next week's brand order.

PHONE AND DESK ORDERS

Take the order while you talk

Build an order at the customer's own price, for shipping or pickup. Any price you change is logged with who changed it.

QUOTES

A quote they accept online

Email a quote the customer reads and accepts from their inbox, with an optional deposit to hold the order.

PAY LINKS

Get paid any way they like

Send a pay link by email, take a card over the phone, charge a saved card, or mark it paid by cash or check.

4 more ways to take an order
Special orders. Track a customer's request from the ask to the purchase order to pickup, with a deposit, and add many at once to a vendor order.
"It's here" holds. When the vendor order arrives, the units are set aside and the customer gets an email.
Drop-ship from the order. One click creates a purchase order that ships straight from the vendor to the customer.
Registry desk and hand-over. Help a gift buyer in store, see what's most wanted, and hand over gifts you've been holding.
10:30 · THE MORNING QUEUE

Overnight orders from two countries and a wine club, one clear list.

Nadia opens the queue, narrows it to magnigift.com.mx orders, and answers a shopper's question from inside the order. Over at Cielo Verde, Tom Reyes checks the scheduled orders desk for the next wine club run and the two cards that failed to charge.

ORDER FILTERS

The view you need, kept

Narrow orders by status, channel and customer. Save the link and it reopens the same view tomorrow.

TIMELINE AND NOTES

The whole story of an order

Every event and every email on one timeline, plus staff notes the customer never sees.

MESSAGE FROM THE ORDER

Reply without hunting

Email the customer from the right department. Their reply comes back into the shared inbox, on the same thread.

6 more tools for the queue
In-store sales view. Filter counter sales by period, register, staff member and payment type.
Resend any email. Send the confirmation, shipped or cancellation email again in one click.
Cancellation requests. Customers ask to cancel; you approve with an automatic refund, or decline.
Change ship-to and re-rate. Fix an address before it ships, then keep, refund or charge the new shipping rate.
Scheduled orders desk. Every subscription and gift calendar, the next 30 days and any failed charges; skip or cancel for the customer.
Abandoned cart dashboard. See open carts, the reminders sent and the sales they won back.
13:00 · PICK AND PACK

Andre walks the Charleston warehouse once and packs a hundred orders.

One pick list, one pass through the aisles, a scanner in hand. A wrong candle is refused before it goes in the box, the hotel order ships in fourteen boxes, and an Asheville order is sent from the Asheville store instead. Every label prints in one go.

BATCH PICK LISTS

One walk, every order

One pick list per location with item totals to tick off, so nobody walks the same aisle twice.

SCAN-TO-PACK

The wrong item never ships

A wrong or extra item is refused on the spot. The order ships with exactly what was scanned.

BATCH LABELS

Thirty labels, one file

Buy labels for many orders at once and print them all from a single 4×6 file.

4 more packing tools
Partial shipments. Ship part of an order from any location; void a shipment and its stock comes back.
Print to any printer. Order tickets, pickup slips, packing slips and gift messages, with this store's printers listed first.
Branded packing slip. Slips with no prices, in the look of the store the order came from.
UPS WorldShip and FedEx files. Export orders for the carrier's own software and bring the tracking numbers back.
16:30 · RETURNS COME BACK

The returns bin fills up after the holidays. Each one takes two minutes.

Andre opens a parcel, marks one bowl chipped and one fine, and the refund works itself out. The shopper chose store credit with a bonus, so the sale stays with Magnigift.

RETURNS QUEUE

Approve or decline in a click

Approve with a prepaid label or decline with a reason. Either way the customer is emailed.

INSPECT AND REFUND

Check it, and the maths is done

Mark each item resellable, damaged or missing, and the refund calculates itself.

STORE CREDIT BONUS

Keep the money in the shop

Refund to a gift card with a bonus percentage on top, or back to the original payments, split the way they were paid.

2 more return tools
Exchanges. Customers swap a size or colour, and the price difference settles automatically.
Refund with smart split. Refund all or part of an order, with the shipping and tax shares suggested for you.
