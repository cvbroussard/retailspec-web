You run several stores and a website, and you are weighing Lightspeed against RetailSpec.

Both run stores, stock and online selling from one system. They are built around different ideas. Lightspeed offers a POS with plans, add-ons and an app market. RetailSpec puts stores, websites, shipping, marketing and brand catalogues in one product. This page sets out the facts so you can decide which fits your business. Every Lightspeed claim links to Lightspeed's own pages.

As of November 2026

Who should stay with Lightspeed

Lightspeed is a mature product with features some stores depend on. If any of these describe you, staying is a sound choice.

  • You run a repair or service counter. Lightspeed sells and tracks service work such as work orders, repairs and alterations from the POS.3 4 RetailSpec does not offer work orders.
  • You order from suppliers on NuORDER. Lightspeed Wholesale lets retailers place supplier orders in the POS, with catalogues of more than 5 million products from over 4,000 suppliers.5
  • You want financing from your POS provider. Lightspeed Capital is listed on its retail plans, in select regions.1
  • You are happy with your setup. If your stores, payments and reports work well today, a move costs time and training. That is a fair reason to stay.

Who RetailSpec is built for

Established retailers with several stores and many brands, selling in stores and online. Often they sell across borders, in more than one currency and language. Often they run a trade program for designers, hotels or other businesses. RetailSpec gives them one catalogue, one stock picture and one customer record across all of it.

What the add-ons add up to

Lightspeed's monthly plan prices are published: Basic $89, Core $149 and Plus $289, each with one register.1 What a multi-store business pays depends on what it adds on top. RetailSpec has not published pricing yet, so this section compares what each one includes.

With Lightspeed, priced or arranged separately

  • Extra locations and registers, quoted by sales.1
  • AI Doc Import on the Basic and Core plans.1
  • Lightspeed Advanced Marketing for tiers, flows and segmentation.12
  • Mailchimp for email campaigns and segmentation.8
  • A monthly fee if you keep another payment processor.13
  • Separate accounts for stores that trade in different currencies.9

With RetailSpec, part of the product

  • POS, storefronts, shipping, marketing email, loyalty and referrals.
  • Brand Library catalogues that arrive written, photographed and barcoded.
  • Several storefronts with their own currency and language.
  • Trade accounts with price lists.
  • Accounting sync and Sam, the console copilot.
  • Payments through your own Stripe account.

Lightspeed prices are the US monthly prices shown on its Retail (X-Series) pricing page. Lightspeed notes that prices can vary and additional service fees may apply.1
