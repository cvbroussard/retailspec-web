22:00 · LOCKING UP

Every register in every store, closed out clean.

A sale was parked at Register 2 in Savannah and never paid. A checkout on magnigift.com was started and dropped. San Miguel closes an hour later on its own clock and gets the same care. RetailSpec clears them all, puts the stock back on sale, and closes out what's long finished.

REGISTER CLEAN-UP

Abandoned register sales voided

A register sale left unpaid for an hour is voided and any payments on it are reversed.

STOCK HOLDS

Held stock lets go

Stock held by an unpaid checkout goes back on sale after two hours, so nothing sits locked up overnight.

AUTO-CLOSE

Old orders wrapped up

Orders shipped long ago are marked delivered, and quiet conversations close themselves.

1 more safe habit
Test orders stay in-house. Emails about test orders go only to your staff, never to shoppers.
00:00 · MIDNIGHT SHOPPERS

Both websites keep selling, and keep following up.

An interior designer left a cart on magnigift.com at 23:10. A gift card was bought weeks ago for a birthday that starts at midnight. The sage throw is back in stock at the Charleston warehouse. Each person hears about it at the right moment.

CART REMINDERS

Gentle cart reminders

Up to three reminders, with the cart repriced to today. They stop the moment the shopper orders.

BACK IN STOCK

"Notify me" that always fires

The email goes out however the stock came back: a delivery, a return or a recount.

GIFT CARDS

Delivered on the day

A gift card bought for a later date is emailed on that date, without anyone pressing send.

3 more things that run on schedule
Subscription orders. Repeat orders create and charge themselves after a heads-up; declined cards retry.
Points expiry. Unused points expire after a warning, and any purchase resets the clock.
Registry completion discount. After the event, the registrant gets your discount on what's left, no code needed.
02:00 · THE QUIET HOURS

The money and the good name of two businesses, watched.

A dispute arrives on a Cielo Verde wine club charge. A Magnigift payout bounces. A campaign to the trade list starts hitting bad addresses. Each one is caught and sent to the right person.

THE BELL

Disputes and payouts flagged

Disputes with deadlines, failed payouts and refunds made outside RetailSpec reach the right people.

CAMPAIGN SAFETY HOLD

Campaigns pause themselves

If bounces or complaints spike, the campaign pauses to protect your sender reputation.

NO DOUBLE-PROCESSING

Everything happens once

Payment events, postings to your books and incoming email are checked for duplicates and retried if they fail.

1 more safeguard
Bounces and complaints. A bounced order email rings the bell; a spam complaint unsubscribes that address.
04:00 · THE WORLD WAKES

Mexico wakes up to a store in Spanish and in pesos.

Before San Miguel opens, the products added to magnigift.com yesterday are already in Spanish on magnigift.com.mx. Peso prices sit on fresh exchange rates, and the new brand import finished while every browser was closed.

TRANSLATIONS

Every language, kept current

EXCHANGE RATES

WALLETS

1 more overnight job
Hands-off catalogue imports. Long brand imports keep going with no browser tab open, and email you when done.
