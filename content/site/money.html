Money power · Payments and books

Two businesses, two currencies, four states. Month end, and your accountant has one question. You already have the answer.

Across a dozen payouts a week, hundreds of supplier bills and tax in several places, you know where the hours go: matching deposits, chasing invoices, re-keying sales into the books. RetailSpec keeps every payment, bill and tax figure in one place and sends a clean daily journal for each store to your accounting system, so the month closes on time.

A pottery maker in Puebla emails a PDF for the Talavera order. RetailSpec reads it, attaches it to the purchase order, flags the one line where the price went up, and spreads the freight and duties into each platter's cost.

South Carolina, Georgia, North Carolina and California are due. The numbers are ready today.
