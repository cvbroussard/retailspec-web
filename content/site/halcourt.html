Meet Halcourt · the retail group in our stories

It started with one shop on Wren Alley.

Claire Whitaker opened a single gift shop in Charleston. Fifteen years later her family's company runs four stores in two countries, two websites, a trade program, a winery and its own distribution centre. This is how a store like yours grows, and what has to change along the way.

The story

Every stage of growth breaks something.

Each step forward for Halcourt came with a new problem. Here is the problem at each stage, and how it was solved.

2009

One shop, one register

Claire opens Magnigift on Wren Alley in Charleston: ceramics, linen, candles and gifts from a handful of brands she loves.

The problemEvery new product typed in by hand, photographed on the counter, priced from a supplier spreadsheet.
TodayBrands arrive from the Brand Library already photographed, described and barcoded.
2014

The website

magnigift.com opens. Online orders start arriving from all over the country.

The problemA second system. The website and the register each had their own stock count, and they never agreed. Items sold twice.
TodayOne catalogue and one stock count for the store and the website.
2016

Halcourt Holdings and the first warehouse

The family forms Halcourt Holdings, takes a warehouse in North Charleston and launches a trade program for interior designers and hotels.

The problemTrade customers on spreadsheets, trade prices in someone's head, purchase orders by email.
TodayTrade accounts with their own price lists, purchase orders built in, and stock moved from warehouse to store with a scan.
2018

A winery joins the family

Halcourt acquires Cielo Verde Cellars, the Ruiz family's winery in Paso Robles, founded in 2004. Elena Ruiz stays on to run it.

The problemA completely different business: age checks, a wine club, cases and pallets for restaurants. Another set of software.
TodayThe winery runs on the same platform, with its own storefronts, its 21+ check, the club and wholesale.
2021 to 2024

Savannah, Asheville, and Mexico

Magnigift opens in Savannah and Asheville, then in San Miguel de Allende with a Spanish website in pesos.

The problemEach new store meant another register setup, another stock count, another currency. Regulars weren't recognised from one store to the next.
TodayEvery store, both countries and both websites on one platform. A customer is known in Savannah and in San Miguel.
Today

One platform, room to grow

A distribution centre with Halcourt's name on it feeds four stores and two websites. Claire reads the week's numbers over coffee, and the next store is already in the plan.

TodayOne login, one support line, one bill, and the whole group in one place.
