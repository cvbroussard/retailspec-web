MON 08:14 · CHECKOUT

Lucía, in Guadalajara, picks a delivery date she can trust.

She's buying a lamp and a throw on magnigift.com.mx, in Spanish. The order will ship from the Charleston warehouse, so the carrier options she sees at checkout are the real ones from there, with an "arrives by" date.

RATES AT CHECKOUT

Real options, not guesses

Customers see real carrier choices, set by where the order will actually ship from.

DELIVERY PROMISES

"Arrives by" you can stand behind

Arrival dates and Fastest or Best value badges, shown only when the estimate is confident.

ADDRESS CHECK

Typos caught, sales kept

The carrier suggests a corrected address. It never blocks the order.

1 more checkout tool
Shipping profiles. Rates by zone, package and weight class, and a choice of which locations ship.
MON 14:00 · THE LABEL

Two boxes to Mexico, one click, insured, customs filled in.

Andre at the warehouse packs the lamp and the throw in separate boxes. He buys both labels from the order, the lamp is insured because it's over Magnigift's set value, and the tariff code and country of origin come straight from the product. That afternoon, Cielo Verde prints its wine club labels from one file. Postage for both comes out of the shipping wallet.

RETAILSPEC SHIPPING

Discounted labels, no extra account

Buy discounted labels from a prepaid shipping wallet, straight from the order. No separate shipping service to sign up for.

MULTI-BOX AND INSURANCE

Big orders, no fuss

Labels for several boxes at once, with saved box sizes and automatic insurance over a value you set.

CUSTOMS DATA

Overseas without the forms

International labels carry the tariff code, country of origin and description straight from the product.

2 more label tools from the order desk
Batch labels, one file. Buy labels for many orders at once and print them from a single 4×6 file.
Scan-to-pack. A wrong or extra item is refused before the box is sealed.
THU 11:20 · DELIVERED

Lucía knew where it was the whole way, across the border.

Tracking emails went out as the parcel moved, in Magnigift's name. Nobody on the order desk had to answer "where's my order?" this week, in English or in Spanish.

TRACKING UPDATES

Tracking that sends itself

Customers get tracking emails as the parcel moves, without anyone copying numbers.

RESEND ANY EMAIL

Lost the email? One click

Send the shipped email again from the order whenever a customer asks.

MESSAGE FROM THE ORDER

Answer from the order itself

Email the customer from the order, and the reply comes back to your shared inbox on the same thread.

2 more tools for parcels on the move
Change ship-to and re-rate. Fix an address before it ships, then keep, refund or charge the new rate.
Partial shipments. Send what's ready from any location and the rest when it arrives.
DAY 12 · THE RETURN

The throw is the wrong shade. She sends it back herself.

Lucía starts the return on magnigift.com.mx, prints the label, and drops it off. The first carrier scan tells the team it's on the way, and the refund waits until Andre has looked at it.

SELF-SERVE RETURNS

Customers start their own returns

Customers start a return within your window and print the label. You refund after inspection.

RETURNS POLICY

Your rules, applied every time

Set the return window, who pays for the label, which reasons get a free return, and a store-credit bonus.

RETURN TRACKING

Know it's on the way

The carrier's first scan moves the return to "in transit" by itself.

2 more return tools from the order desk
Inspect and refund. Mark items resellable, damaged or missing, and the refund calculates itself.
Exchanges. Swap a size or colour, and the price difference settles automatically.
