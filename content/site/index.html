The patchwork problem

Somewhere past your second store, the software starts running you.

Growing retailers end up with a register system, a website platform, an email app, a shipping app, a loyalty app and an accounting plug-in. Each one has its own login, its own bill, its own support line and its own copy of your customers. Every copy drifts, and your team spends the week keeping them in step.

The Brand Library

You sell other companies' products. Someone has to set each one up.

See the Brand Library →
  1. 1
    Who RetailSpec is for

    RetailSpec is built for businesses that sell products made by many different brands, in their stores, on their websites and to trade customers.

  2. 2
    The work you already know

    Before a product can sell anywhere, someone on your team sets it up: the name, the photos, the description, the barcode, every size and colour. Then again for the website, then again for the next brand, and again whenever a brand changes something.

  3. 3
    The part nobody sees

    Every other shop that carries the same brand is doing exactly the same work, by hand, for the same products.

  4. 4
    What RetailSpec does instead

    We set up each brand's products once, from the brand's own photos, descriptions and barcodes, and keep them current. That shared catalogue is the Brand Library.

  5. 5
    What it means for you

    Turn on a brand you carry and its products arrive ready to sell in every store and on every website. You add your prices and your own words.
