07:00 · GETTING READY

The autumn banner goes up on both storefronts before the stores open.

Priya Raman, Magnigift's web lead, clicks the offer banner on magnigift.com, types the new line, then does the same in Spanish on magnigift.com.mx. She sends Claire Whitaker a preview link. Claire says yes from her phone, and both are published. If they change their minds, last week's version is one click away.

WEB STUDIO

Change your website yourself

Click the header, menus, offer banner or home page sections on the live page and change them, on your own domain, with no developer.

DRAFT AND PUBLISH

Draft, share, publish, roll back

Every change starts as a draft. Share a 7-day preview link, publish when it's right, or roll back.

PER STOREFRONT

Each website set up its own way

Currency, tax-inclusive prices, language, the store that fulfils, and prepay or invoiced, set per storefront. Dollars and English on one site, pesos and Spanish on the other.

7 more ways to get the website ready
Header, menus and offer banner. Set menus, rotating offer lines and a strip for your sister stores.
Home page stack. Put the home page sections in order, starting from a preset.
Coming soon switch. Show "Coming soon" to the public while preview links show the real store.
What each channel sells. Give each channel its own products and price lists, with the brand's list price as a fallback.
Storefront languages. New languages translate as pages open and stay hidden from search until you publish them.
Translation glossary. Approve machine translations, write your own, and lock the terms you care about.
Country picker. One choice sets currency, language and policies, picked for the visitor automatically.
10:15 · FIRST VISITOR

Someone in Asheville searched "fig candle near me". They found the Asheville store.

Magnigift's products show up in Google and shopping feeds with prices that match the shelf in each store. A visitor lands, types "fig", and sees the candle, the brand and the category in one glance. Over on the Cielo Verde Cellars site, the age gate asks first.

PRODUCT FEEDS

Found where people look

Product feeds for Google, Microsoft and Meta, plus a guide that helps AI assistants recommend your products correctly.

SMART SEARCH

Search that finishes the thought

Suggestions as they type, with matching brands and categories beside the results.

SAM GIFT CONCIERGE

"It's for my sister, she loves cooking"

Shoppers describe who they're buying for and Sam, our AI assistant, suggests gifts from your stock, under your brand.

5 more ways visitors become shoppers
Google local inventory. Show what's on your shelf right in Google search.
Two-tier reviews. Verified-buyer reviews plus credited brand reviews; review links need no sign-in.
"Ask for this." Shoppers send someone a link to the exact item they'd love.
Your analytics tags. GA4, Tag Manager and the Meta pixel, behind the shopper's cookie choice, with purchases sent from our side.
Age gate. Confirm age on entry and at checkout (21 in the US, 18 elsewhere) while search engines can still read the page.
13:30 · THE BASKET

One candle in the cart. The website suggests the second, in dollars or pesos.

The buy-two candle deal runs on both websites just as it does in all four stores. The cart says "Add 1 more to save 15%", and the shopper adds an engraved board. Later, an interior designer in the trade program signs in and sees her trade price list.

DEALS

The same deals, online and in store

Buy X get Y, mix and match, tiers and spend-and-get, set up once and honoured everywhere.

DEAL COACHING

A nudge at the right moment

"Add 1 more" and "Spend $12 more" messages in the cart show shoppers how close they are to a deal.

PERSONALIZATION

Monograms and engraving

Add personalisation fields with an upcharge, and mark personalised items as final sale.

7 more ways to build a bigger basket
Promotions library. Every season and sale in one list; switch many on at once, shift dates, or copy for next year.
Promotion editor. Edit the hero, badge and layout as shoppers see them, and preview how the site looks on a future date.
Discount codes. Percent or fixed codes with limits, dates and a minimum spend.
Price lists. Fixed, percent-of-list-price or custom lists, with dates, currency and quantity breaks.
Bundles. Sell items together without stocking a bundle; each item lands as its own line.
Gift wrap and messages. Wrap styles priced per item or per package, with messages and hidden prices per line.
Purchase limits. Minimums, maximums and sold-in-multiples per product or channel.
16:40 · CHECKOUT

Two taps to pay, even for the board still on its way to the Charleston warehouse.

The large board is out of stock, so the page offers it on backorder with a ship-around date. A visitor from Norway can still order: Magnigift quotes the shipping later and sends a link to pay. A Savannah local chooses to pay when she picks up.

EXPRESS CHECKOUT

Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link

Set up automatically on each of your domains, so returning shoppers pay in a couple of taps.

KEEP SELLING

Pre-order, backorder, made to order

Keep selling when you're out, with a ship-around date, product by product.

QUOTE THEN PAY

Orders from anywhere

Take orders to countries without set rates, then quote the shipping and send a pay link.

2 more ways to pay
Pay at the counter. Order online, pay when you pick up in store.
Gift cards. Digital and physical cards that never expire, scheduled delivery, and a gift card desk to find and adjust any card.
21:47 · THE LAST ORDER

A wedding guest buys from a Charleston registry. The couple won't see it until the big day.

Jonah scans the QR code on the invitation card, picks the serving board from the registry, and it's held for the couple in surprise mode. Then he signs in to reorder his own candles. Across the company, next month's Cielo Verde wine club shipments wait for their heads-up email.

GIFT REGISTRY

Registries guests can find

A short code, QR invitation cards, surprise mode, held gifts and a completion discount for the couple.

SHOPPER ACCOUNT

Everything in one account

Orders, buy again, returns, messages, saved cards, rewards, a gift card wallet and preferences.

SCHEDULED ORDERS

Orders that come back on their own

Subscribe-and-save, box-of-the-month and gift calendars, charged after a heads-up. A winery can run its club shipments this way.

2 more ways shoppers come back
Four list types. Favorites, wishlist, registry and project list; hearts saved while signed out move into the account.
Ask us to order it. Shoppers request an item you don't stock and follow it until it's ready.
