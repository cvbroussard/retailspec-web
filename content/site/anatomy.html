Online stores · how they’re built

The anatomy of a store that sells.

Every RetailSpec storefront is built the same way: a proven structure first, filled by your own data, styled last. Here is every part of a home page, top to bottom, what it does for your shoppers, and how little you have to do to make it work.

Complete on day oneBuilt to convertFills itself from your data
The method

Structure first. Your data fills it. Style comes last.

1 · Structure

A proven layout for every page

Each kind of page has the same parts in the same order, the order shoppers expect, from the top bar to the footer.

2 · Your data

Filled by what you already have

Your catalogue, your brands, your promotions, your shops and your settings fill every part. Nothing waits for someone to build a page.

3 · Style

Your look, applied last

Colours, type, photography and layout choices are applied on top, so a store looks like you without ever breaking what makes it sell.

The home page, top to bottom

Twelve parts, each with a job.

Shown here with Magnigift, the gift retailer in our stories. Every RetailSpec store has the same anatomy.

  1. 1
    Sibling bar

    Your other doors

    A slim bar above everything: your other websites, and your nearest shop with today’s hours.

    Why it sells
    A shopper who learns there’s a shop ten minutes away, open until six, has a reason to visit, or to order online and pick up.
    What fills it
    Your storefronts’ addresses and each shop’s hours, from Store details.
    What you do
    Nothing. It appears once your shops have their hours.
  2. 2
    Announcement bar

    The one line everyone reads

    A single rotating line: the offer of the moment, free shipping, a holiday deadline.

    Why it sells
    It answers “is there a deal?” before the question is asked, on every page.
    What fills it
    Your live promotions and your shipping settings.
    What you do
    Switch it on. It fills itself from there.
  3. 3
    Header

    Find it, keep it, buy it

    Logo, search, account, favourites and bag, the same on every page.

    Why it sells
    Search covers products, brands and categories, so someone who types a brand name lands on that brand, not a long list.
    What fills it
    Your logo and storefront settings.
    What you do
    Upload a logo.
  4. 4
    Main menu

    Shop by what it is

    Your departments as menus that open to their sub-categories.

    Why it sells
    Shoppers who browse need a map. The menu builds and orders itself from your categories, biggest departments first, and you can still reorder it.
    What fills it
    Your categories and what sells in them.
    What you do
    Nothing.
  5. 5
    Moments row

    Shop by when and who

    Live seasons, holidays and occasions, then your brands.

    Why it sells
    Gift shoppers think in occasions. Links appear and leave on their own dates, so the store always feels current.
    What fills it
    Your promotions’ dates.
    What you do
    Create a promotion with dates. That’s all.
  6. 6
    Hero

    One message, one button

    The first thing a shopper sees: one image, one headline, one call to action. It loads before anything else.

    Why it sells
    A single clear message converts better than a slideshow nobody waits for.
    What fills it
    Your top live promotion, else your own year-round hero.
    What you do
    Approve a hero image, generated from your store’s own story.
  7. 7
    Promotion stream

    What’s happening now

    Each live promotion with its products, down the page.

    Why it sells
    This is how a store feels alive. Promotions arrive on their start date and leave on their end date, with no one editing the page.
    What fills it
    Your promotions: dated, or evergreen like “Hostess gifts”.
    What you do
    Approve the calendar Sam drafts.
  8. 8
    Ways in

    For people who know what they want

    Shop by category, our brands, new arrivals, best sellers.

    Why it sells
    Not everyone arrives for the season. These rows give every shopper a quick path in.
    What fills it
    Your catalogue and your sales.
    What you do
    Nothing.
  9. 9
    Your story

    Why you, not a marketplace

    Who you are, in a few lines and one photo.

    Why it sells
    Independent retailers win on trust. A real story and a real shop front are what a marketplace can’t copy.
    What fills it
    Your channel’s own story, drafted by Sam and approved by you.
    What you do
    Read the draft and approve it.
  10. 10
    Reasons to buy here

    The promises you already keep

    Free shipping over a threshold, easy returns, gift wrap, pickup in your town.

    Why it sells
    These are drawn from your settings, so they’re always true. Change a threshold and the store updates.
    What fills it
    Your shipping, returns and gift settings.
    What you do
    Switch it on.
  11. 11
    Sign-up

    A reason to come back

    An email sign-up with your welcome offer and the consent wording stored.

    Why it sells
    A subscriber is a shopper you can reach again without paying for an ad.
    What fills it
    Your welcome offer and email flows.
    What you do
    Switch it on.
  12. 12
    Footer

    The answers people look for last

    Contact, policies, your trade programme, your shops, social.

    Why it sells
    Shoppers check returns and contact details just before they buy. Clear answers keep the sale.
    What fills it
    Your policies, contact details and storefronts.
    What you do
    Nothing beyond your settings.
What we don’t let you do, and why

A few rules that protect the sale.

No rearranging the pageThe order is the order shoppers expect. You switch parts on and off; you never have to arrange them.
One headline per pageSearch engines and shoppers both read the page better with one clear headline.
The hero loads firstThe most important image arrives before anything else, on every phone.
Nothing empty ever showsA part with nothing to say steps aside, and the next one moves up.
Offers are always trueFree shipping and returns come from your settings, so the page can’t advertise an old offer.
Promotions leave on timeEvery promotion leaves every part of the store on its end date. No one has to remember to take it down.
The difference

Themes give you the freedom to make a store pretty. We give you a store that’s complete, built to convert, and kept that way.

When your catalogue grows, your promotions change and your season turns, the store keeps up on its own, because every part knows where its content comes from.
