RetailSpec · unified platform

One code base. It makes every call.

Everything RetailSpec does is one code base. The integrations that reach payment networks, carriers, accounting systems and AI models are written, owned and run inside it. The outside services sit at the end of the line. They answer our calls; they don't run your store.

Who makes the call is the difference

An integration and a bolted-on app can look alike on a feature list. Their wiring runs in opposite directions.

RetailSpec integration

Our code calls out

  • The logic lives in RetailSpec. The outside service only answers.
  • Your data stays in your own RetailSpec database. Nothing is copied to a third party's app.
  • One login, one support line, one bill.
  • When we update RetailSpec, the integration is updated and tested with it.
  • Keys are sealed in our encrypted vault, and every AI call is metered in one ledger.
Bolted-on app

Someone else's code calls in

  • The logic lives in another company's code base, outside your platform.
  • It reads your store through an access key and keeps its own copy of your customers and orders.
  • Separate subscription, separate login, separate support queue.
  • A platform update can break it, and neither vendor owns the fix.
  • Every app you add is another company with access to your data.

Outside services can also notify us when something happens on their side, such as a payment settling or a parcel being scanned. That notice lands in the RetailSpec integration layer, which checks its signature, and our code decides what happens next.
